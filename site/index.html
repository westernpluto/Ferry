Sign with your certificate

Everyone signs in their own copy of Ferry on GitHub, so your certificate and app stay in your account. Your files are encrypted in this browser before they're sent. Takes about 5–10 minutes.

Don't have one? Copy Ferry to your GitHub account. Then, in your copy: Settings › Pages › Source: GitHub Actions, and open the Actions tab once to turn workflows on.

A private link is a secret address only you get. After it's removed, nothing signed is left online.

Create a fine-grained token: Repository access › Only select repositories › your copy. Permissions: Actions and Secrets set to Read and write, Pages set to Read. A 7-day expiry is plenty. It's only used in this tab and never saved.

    After installing

    1. Wait for the icon to finish loading on the Home Screen.
    2. Development build: turn on Developer Mode in Settings › Privacy & Security, then restart.
    3. Open Ferry and set up signing and the install certificate in Settings. Everything after that works offline.
